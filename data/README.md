# Shared course database

`course.js` contains the exact `SEED_CONTENT` object from the uploaded Financial Management Study Companion. `notebook-source.js` retains every chapter, summary, problem, solution, quotation, note, slide, video reference, card, quiz, and data-enrichment pass from `FIN 303 · Notebook Study Guide (Chapters 3–8).html`, including its final section-renumbering pass. The only helper change is making `st1QBox(want, chapter)` receive its chapter explicitly.

`database.js` is the single registry that connects these source objects. Its `DB.course` and `DB.chapters` combine Chapters `ch1`–`ch8` without adding invented material to the original Companion `CONTENT`, which still covers Chapters 1–6. Original objectives use `lo1.1` style identifiers. Notes, homework, cards, quizzes, formulas, and summaries all carry `chapterId` and `learningObjectiveIds`. Source objects remain unmodified.

Notebook numbers follow the updated source labels. `NOTEBOOK_OBJECTIVE_MAP` records combined topics and the Chapter 6 topics supplied separately from Companion objectives. Notebook topics now numbered 6.8 and 6.9 retain their stable IDs `lo6.7-notebook` and `lo6.8-notebook`. Chapters 7–8 add 20 supplemental objectives, one per Notebook section, with identifiers such as `lo7.1-notebook`. All supplemental topics carry explicit provenance and are not presented as supplied Companion textbook objectives. Where the source does not identify an exact objective for a card or question, the registry records a chapter-level association instead of inventing an exact link.

Normalized records use namespaced IDs such as `note:ch3:3.1a`, `homework:ch3:p31`, `quiz:notebook:ch3:0`, and `card:notebook:ch3:0`. `homeworkIds` connects each note to its immediately related work; homework records link back through `notebookSectionIds`.

`provenance.json` records upload hashes, source-content hashes, and source counts. Intentional content edits require reviewing and updating the relevant fingerprint. Feature-only changes should leave source hashes unchanged. Formulas are indexed from concept fields, textbook formula blocks, notebook formulas, worked steps, exam reviews, and formula cards; repeated formulas in distinct source contexts retain their provenance.

The combined registry contains 8 chapters, 64 objectives (42 original and 22 supplemental), 57 Notebook sections, 114 homework records, 237 quizzes, 481 flashcards, 510 formula records, and 126 summaries. The Notebook source contributes 71 worked problems, 39 conceptual items, 4 key-term sets, 252 cards, 86 quizzes, and 150 slides.
